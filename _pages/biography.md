## Biography

I am a graduate student in Journalism at Nanjing University. My research interests include journalism studies, political communication, and the relationship between media, politics, and society.

I am particularly interested in how news media participate in the construction of social and political realities, as well as how audiences' media use, emotions, and political attitudes interact with one another.
